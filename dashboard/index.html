Circuit → Environment Coupling

The backend interprets physical sensor changes and derives the digital-twin environment.

01 — Temperature

NTC water temperature influences the thermal state and ice model.

02 — Pressure

BME280 atmospheric pressure contributes to environmental stress calculations.

03 — Motion

MPU6050 acceleration and gyro values influence the simulated buoy disturbance and trajectory.

04 — Polar Rules

Backend rules keep the generated environment inside the defined polar operating envelope.

05 — Risk

Environmental severity and telemetry anomalies are converted into operational risk.

06 — Autonomy

Sampling and energy behavior adapt according to the current mission state.
